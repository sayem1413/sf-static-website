Suborna Akter (Sonali)
Chairperson
Subaita Foundation
✦ Chairperson's Message "

Welcome to Subaita Foundation

At Subaita Foundation, we believe that true social development begins with empowering people through knowledge, skills, and opportunities. Our mission is to uplift women, youth, and marginalized communities by promoting education, vocational training, entrepreneurship, legal awareness, and humanitarian support.

As a woman, a mother, and a social worker, I strongly believe that motherhood is not a barrier to leadership — it is a source of strength. Every woman deserves the opportunity to become self-reliant, contribute to society, and achieve her full potential.

Our vision is to build an inclusive and compassionate Bangladesh where every individual has the opportunity to grow with dignity and confidence. Together with our dedicated volunteers, partners, and supporters, we are committed to creating sustainable change that positively impacts lives across the country.

I warmly invite you to join our journey of service, empowerment, and hope. Together, we can build a brighter future for generations to come.

— Suborna Akter (Sonali), Chairperson, Subaita Foundation

Khairul Amin

Subaita Foundation
✦ General Secretary's Message "

Welcome to Subaita Foundation

At Subaita Foundation, we are committed to transforming compassion into meaningful action. Our mission is to empower women, youth, and marginalized communities through education, skills development, entrepreneurship, humanitarian assistance, and community engagement.

We believe that sustainable development is achieved when individuals are equipped with the knowledge, confidence, and opportunities to become self-reliant. Every training program, awareness campaign, and social initiative we undertake is designed to create lasting impact and strengthen communities from the grassroots level.

As General Secretary, I am proud to work alongside our dedicated volunteers, Executive Committee members, partners, and supporters who share a common vision of building a more inclusive, equitable, and prosperous Bangladesh. Their commitment and collective efforts continue to inspire positive change in the lives of countless people.

We warmly invite individuals, organizations, and development partners to join our journey. Together, we can expand opportunities, inspire hope, and create sustainable solutions that improve lives and contribute to a stronger and more compassionate society.

Thank you for your trust, support, and belief in our mission.

— Khairul Amin, General Secretary, Subaita Foundation
